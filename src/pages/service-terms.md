These terms apply to the commercial and residential cleaning services provided by Jamal Cleaning Services Ltd. ("we",
"us") to you, our customer. Your quote, and any signed service agreement, sets out the details of your job (scope,
schedule and price). If your quote or signed agreement says something different from these terms, the quote or
agreement applies.

## Quotes

- Quotes are based on the information you give us and, where needed, a site walkthrough. If the site, the scope or the
  condition is different from what was described, we will tell you and agree any change in price or scope with you before
  doing the extra work.
- A quote is valid for the period stated on it.
- Work is booked once you accept the quote.

## Scheduling and changes

- We agree the date, time and frequency of the service with you, including after-hours or overnight cleaning.
- If you need to cancel or reschedule, please tell us as early as possible. Any cancellation terms or fees are set out in
  your quote or agreement.
- We will let you know as soon as possible if we need to reschedule, for example because of severe weather or an
  emergency.

## Access and site conditions

To let us do the job safely and well, you agree to:

- give us access to the site at the agreed time, including keys, access cards, alarm codes or a contact person on site;
- provide running water, electricity and lighting where we work;
- tell us about anything that affects safety, such as hazards, fragile or valuable items, special surfaces, or pets;
- put away or secure cash, jewellery, confidential documents and other valuables;
- make sure the areas to be cleaned are reasonably clear.

We keep keys, access cards and alarm codes secure and use them only to provide your service. We return them when the
service ends.

We may decline or stop work in conditions that are unsafe for our cleaners, such as biohazards, pests, mould, or
structural or electrical hazards, or where the work needs a licensed specialist.

## Products and equipment

We bring our own cleaning products and equipment unless we agree otherwise. If you would like us to use particular
products, or to avoid some, tell us before the service. Let us know about any allergies or sensitivities.

## Payment

Prices, invoicing and payment terms are set out in your quote or agreement. Applicable taxes (such as GST) are added
to the price unless the quote says they are included.

## If something is not right

We want you to be happy with every clean. If something was missed or does not meet the agreed standard, tell us as soon
as possible, ideally with photos, and we will arrange to come back and re-clean the areas concerned.

## Damage and breakage

Please report any damage you believe we caused as soon as possible, with photos and details, so we can look into it
while the facts are fresh. Where we are responsible, we will repair, replace or pay for the item, as is fair. We are not
responsible for:

- normal wear and tear, or the existing condition of surfaces and items;
- damage caused by faulty or improperly installed items, fixtures or finishes;
- items that were not secured, or hazards we were not told about.

## Our cleaners

Our cleaners are trained and supervised by us. Please speak to us, not to our staff directly, about changes to your
service or any concerns. You agree not to hire our cleaners directly for cleaning work during your service and for a
reasonable period after it ends, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

WorkSafeBC documents you need in a written service agreement.
