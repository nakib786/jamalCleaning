Jamal Cleaning Services Ltd. ("Jamal Cleaning Services", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what
personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to
our website (jmcleaningservice.ca), our quote and contact forms, our newsletter, and the information we handle when we
provide cleaning services.

We follow British Columbia's _Personal Information Protection Act_ (PIPA) and, where it applies, the federal _Personal
Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act_ (PIPEDA). Our marketing emails follow Canada's Anti-Spam
Legislation (CASL).

## Who is responsible for your information

Our Privacy Officer is responsible for how we handle personal information and for answering your questions and
requests. Contact the Privacy Officer at [info@jmcleaningservice.ca](mailto:info@jmcleaningservice.ca) or
(236) 331-7323.

## What we collect

- **Quote requests and contact messages:** your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in,
  and anything you write in your message.
- **Newsletter sign-ups:** your email address.
- **Messages you send us** by WhatsApp, phone, text or email, including any photos you share.
- **Service information:** the address of the property or site we clean, access instructions (for example alarm codes,
  keys or access cards), contact people on site, scheduling details, and billing information for our customers.
- **Technical information:** like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data when you visit (for
  example your IP address, browser type and the pages requested). We use it only to run and secure the website.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on our website, and we do not sell personal information.

## Why we use it

We use personal information to:

- answer your questions and prepare quotes;
- schedule, provide and follow up on our cleaning services, and invoice for them;
- keep a record of our customers and enquiries;
- send our newsletter and occasional news and offers by email (see "Email marketing" below);
- protect our forms from spam and abuse, and keep our website secure;
- meet our legal, tax and insurance obligations.

We collect only what we need for these purposes, and we will ask for your consent before using your information for a
new purpose.

## Consent

By sending us a form, message or request, you consent to us using your information for the purposes above. You can
withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, subject to legal or contractual limits (for example, we must keep
invoices for tax purposes). If you withdraw consent, we may not be able to provide some services.

## Email marketing

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we add your email address to our mailing list. When you request a quote, we may
also send you occasional news and offers by email, as permitted by CASL for people who have made an enquiry. Every
marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to unsubscribe you at any time. We process
unsubscribe requests within 10 business days.

## Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as much as they need:

- **Cloudflare** hosts our website, delivers our form notifications by email, and checks forms for spam with its
  Turnstile service.
- **Wix** stores our customer contacts, form submissions and mailing list, and sends our marketing emails.
- **WhatsApp (Meta)** carries the messages you choose to send us on WhatsApp.
- **Google Maps** shows the map on our contact page. Google may set its own cookies when the map loads; see Google's
  privacy policy.
- Our accountant, insurer, payment providers and professional advisers, when needed.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, property or safety.

Some of these providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be
subject to the laws of that country and accessed by its authorities. We choose providers that protect information with
safeguards comparable to ours.

## How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) on our website,
spam protection on our forms, access limited to the staff who need it, and secure storage of keys, access cards and alarm
codes. Our cleaners are trained to keep information they see at a customer's home or business confidential.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires (for
example, tax records). When we no longer need it, we delete or securely destroy it. Quote requests that do not lead to
work are deleted when they are no longer useful to us.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we have used it;
- give you a copy of it;
- correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
- stop sending you marketing emails, or delete your information when we no longer need to keep it.

Send your request to [info@jmcleaningservice.ca](mailto:info@jmcleaningservice.ca). We will reply within 30 days, as
PIPA requires. We may need to confirm your identity first.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the
[Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia](https://www.oipc.bc.ca/).

## Cookies

Our own website does not set cookies. Cloudflare Turnstile (our spam check) and the Google Maps map on the contact page
are provided by those companies and may use their own cookies or similar technologies to work.

## Children

Our website and services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Significant
changes will be posted on this page.
